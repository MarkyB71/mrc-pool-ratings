This is a test version of the MRC Pool Club rating system. Here's how it measures up against the original brief.

Done

  • Player details: name, rating, matches, wins, losses, win %, highest rating, division, match history and head-to-heads
  • New players start at 400 and can't drop below 250
  • Expected score and rating change use the Elo formula
  • K-factor of 40, 25 or 15 depending on matches played
  • Divisions update automatically
  • Saving one result updates ratings, records, head-to-heads, history and the table
  • League table with rank, rating, last change, played, wins, losses, win % and division
  • Stats: highest-rated, biggest gain, longest winning streak, most matches
  • Head-to-head between any two players
  • Match history showing the rating change after every result
  • Rating graph over time for each player
  • Player profile pages
  • Admin area protected by a PIN
  • Public leaderboard anyone can view
  • Live updates every 20 seconds
  • Export the table and results to Excel
  • Works on phones
  • Built so new features can be added later
  • Current club standings loaded from the website

Coming later

  • Tournament management
  • League management (seasons)
  • Handicap calculations
  • Qualification tracking for events
  • QR code player check-in
  • Installable phone app
  • Push notifications
  • Export to PDF

Needs your decision

  • When two players have different K-factors, should we use the average of both (so the winner gains exactly what the loser loses), or should each player use their own K?
  • Only the totals are on the website, so past match-by-match results aren't loaded. Is there a spreadsheet of past results to import?

Rating rules

When the two players have different K-factors:

Start 400 · floor 250 · K 40 for 0–10 matches, 25 for 11–25, 15 for 26+. Changes are rounded to whole points. Changing a rule recalculates every result from the start.
